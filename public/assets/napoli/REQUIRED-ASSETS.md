# Required photographic layers

The animation is paused pending these real photographic PNG/WebP images with transparent backgrounds:

1. `dough.webp`: top-down dough base.
2. `sauce.webp`: tomato sauce alone, aligned to the dough center.
3. `mozzarella.webp`: individual Fior di Latte pieces, or an atlas with piece rectangles.
4. `anchovies.webp`: individual fillets, or an atlas.
5. `capers.webp`: individual capers, or an atlas.
6. `oregano.webp`: oregano flakes alone.
7. `oil.webp`: olive oil drizzle alone.

Use a consistent top-down camera, lighting, scale and canvas center; 1200×1200 pixels is suitable. Exclude table, plate, captions and previous toppings from ingredient layers. An optional `plate.webp` can supply a photographic plate. The dough may be composited over a plain dark plate shape.

The seven-panel image in `pic/` remains the visual reference. Its cumulative perspective scenes cannot serve as independent layers. These ingredient photos can also supply builder drop animations.

Separate menu photos needed: Pepperoni, Pastrami, Beef Bacon, Spicy Honey Diavola, Marinara.
